How to play

carry the last lantern through the dark
  1. Move. W A S D or the arrow keys. You can also hold the mouse or your finger where you want to go.
  2. The lantern fights for you. It fires on its own. Your job is to stay alive and stay out of reach of the shades.
  3. Dash. Press Space or Shift (or right-click) to dash. You cannot be hurt while dashing.
  4. Gather light. Shades drop glowing shards. Collect them to level up, then pick one of three gifts: a new weapon, or a stronger one.
  5. Three realms. Survive until the guardian arrives, then undo it. Red circles on the ground are danger. Gold is yours.
  6. Pick-ups. A golden bell is a free gift, a blue horseshoe pulls in all the light, a red orb is a bell bomb, and a green drop heals you.
  7. The Shrine. Every run earns motes of light. Spend them at the Shrine on the title screen for upgrades that last.
  8. Win, then keep going. After the third guardian you can dive into the endless dark. Esc pauses.
